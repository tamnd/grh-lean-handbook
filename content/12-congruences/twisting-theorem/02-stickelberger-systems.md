## Stickelberger systems

A Stickelberger system is a family of elements $\Theta_Q$ of group rings, one for each finite set $Q$ of places, whose values at characters change by Euler factors when $Q$ grows. It is the $\ell$-adic form of a family of $L$-values. This section gives the definition and its first properties. All results are in [StickelbergerSystems.lean](lean:New/Congruences/Selmer/StickelbergerSystems.lean#L1).

Background used: characters for $Q$ and auxiliary sets (`New.IsFromU1`, `New.IsAuxiliary`, [auxiliary primes](/chapter/tied-traces/#auxiliary-primes)); the Artin map from the finite ideles onto the abelianized Galois group ([the Artin map and the second inequality](/chapter/class-field-theory/#the-artin-map-and-the-second-inequality)); Hilbert 90 for finite ideles (`New.hilbert90_finite_idele`, [base change and odd rank](/chapter/building-representations/#base-change-and-odd-rank)); Frobenius elements `New.IsFrob` ([finite places, decomposition and Frobenius](/chapter/cm-fields/#finite-places-decomposition-and-frobenius)).

### Characters for a set of places

**Lemma.** Let $\chi$ and $\chi'$ be characters for $I$ with respect to $S$. Then $\chi$ is a character for every $I' \supseteq I$, $f \circ \chi$ is one for every homomorphism $f$ of monoids, the trivial character is one, and $\chi \chi'$ is one. If $I'$ is auxiliary for $S$, so is every $I \subseteq I'$.

Lean: `New.IsFromU1.mono`, `New.IsFromU1.map`, `New.IsFromU1.one`, `New.IsFromU1.mul`, `New.IsAuxiliary.mono` (New/Congruences/Selmer/StickelbergerSystems.lean).

*Proof.* Let $\eta$ be the idele character of $\chi$. An idele that is $\equiv 1$ at the places of $I'$ is $\equiv 1$ at those of $I$, so $\eta$ kills it; this gives the first claim. For $f \circ \chi$ take $f \circ \eta$, for $1$ take $1$, and for $\chi\chi'$ take $\eta\eta'$; each condition on $\eta$ is kept. The three conditions of an auxiliary set are conditions on each place, so they pass to subsets. $\square$

### The class groups $\mathrm{Cl}_Q$

**Definition.** Let $Q$ be a finite set of places. The group $\mathrm{Cl}_Q$ is the group of finite ideles $u$ with $u \bar u = 1$, modulo the subgroup generated by the norm-one ideles that are principal and the norm-one ideles that are units everywhere and $\equiv 1$ at the places of $Q$. For a finite idele $y$, $\mathrm{cl}_Q(y)$ is the class of $y/\bar y$.

Lean: `New.Selmer.U1ClassGroup`, `New.Selmer.divStarClass` (New/Congruences/Selmer/StickelbergerSystems.lean).

**Lemma.** The map $\mathrm{cl}_Q$ is surjective.

Lean: `New.Selmer.divStarClass_surjective` (New/Congruences/Selmer/StickelbergerSystems.lean).

*Proof.* Let $u$ have $u \bar u = 1$, and let $\sigma$ be complex conjugation, of order $2$. Then $\prod_{i < 2} \sigma^i(u) = \bar u u = 1$, so Hilbert 90 gives an invertible $y$ with $u \bar y = y$. Then $y \cdot \overline{y^{-1}} = u \bar y \bar y^{-1} = u$. So $u = y'/\bar{y'}$ with $y' = y$. $\square$

**Definition.** For a class $c \in \mathrm{Cl}_Q$, choose an idele $y$ with $\mathrm{cl}_Q(y) = c$ and an element $\mathrm{lift}(c) \in G$ whose image in the abelianization is $\mathrm{Art}(y)$. For a function $f$ on $G$ with values in a module $R$ and an element $a = \sum_c a_c [c]$ of the group ring of $\mathrm{Cl}_Q$, put $f(a) = \sum_c a_c f(\mathrm{lift}(c))$.

Lean: `New.Selmer.galoisLift`, `New.Selmer.groupRingEval` (New/Congruences/Selmer/StickelbergerSystems.lean).

**Remark (Formalization).** The lift uses `Function.surjInv` and `Quotient.out`, so it is a choice. For a general $f$ the value $f(a)$ depends on the choice. For a character for $Q$ it does not, by the next lemma. The source only evaluates such characters, and the trivial character.

**Lemma.** Let $\chi$ be a character for $Q$ (with respect to any $S$), and let $\mathrm{Art}(y)$ be the image of $g$. Then $\chi(\mathrm{lift}(\mathrm{cl}_Q(y))) = \chi(g)$. A linear map $L$ commutes with the evaluation: $L(f(a)) = (L \circ f)(a)$. For a sum $\sum_{i \in s} c_i [u_i]$, the value is $\sum_{i \in s} c_i f(\mathrm{lift}(u_i))$.

Lean: `New.IsFromU1.apply_galoisLift`, `New.Selmer.map_groupRingEval`, `New.Selmer.groupRingEval_sum_single` (New/Congruences/Selmer/StickelbergerSystems.lean).

*Proof.* Let $\eta$ be the idele character of $\chi$. On norm-one ideles, $\eta$ kills the principal ones and the units $\equiv 1$ at $Q$, so it factors through a character $\eta'$ of $\mathrm{Cl}_Q$ with $\eta'(\mathrm{cl}_Q(x)) = \eta(x/\bar x)$. Let $z$ be the chosen idele of the class $\mathrm{cl}_Q(y)$. Then $\chi(\mathrm{lift}) = \eta(z/\bar z) = \eta'(\mathrm{cl}_Q(z)) = \eta'(\mathrm{cl}_Q(y)) = \eta(y/\bar y) = \chi(g)$. The other two claims hold since the evaluation is a linear combination. $\square$

### Euler factors

**Definition.** For a list $e_1, \dots, e_k$ of integers, an element $a$ of a field and a natural number $q$,
$$\mathrm{Eul}(a, q) = \prod_{i=1}^{k} \prod_{\epsilon = \pm 1} (1 - a^{\epsilon} q^{-e_i}).$$

Lean: `New.Selmer.eulerFactor` (New/Congruences/Selmer/StickelbergerSystems.lean).

**Lemma.** For the list $e :: \mathrm{exps}$, $\mathrm{Eul}(a, q) = (1 - a q^{-e})(1 - a^{-1} q^{-e})\, \mathrm{Eul}_{\mathrm{exps}}(a, q)$. For a sign $\delta$, $\mathrm{Eul}(a^\delta, q) = \mathrm{Eul}(a, q)$.

Lean: `New.Selmer.eulerFactor_cons`, `New.Selmer.eulerFactor_zpow_units` (New/Congruences/Selmer/StickelbergerSystems.lean).

*Proof.* The first claim is the definition for one more element. For $\delta = -1$ the two factors of each pair change places. $\square$

### The definition

**Definition (Stickelberger system).** Let $S_0$ be a finite set of places and $g_0 \in G$. A Stickelberger system for $S_0$ and $g_0$ consists of:

1. a continuous character $\vartheta \colon G \to \bar{\mathbb{Q}}_\ell^\times$, trivial on the inertia groups at the places outside $S_0$, with $\vartheta(g_0) \neq 1$;
2. a list of integers, the *exponents*;
3. for every finite set $Q$ of places, an element $\Theta_Q$ of the group ring of $\mathrm{Cl}_Q$ over $\bar{\mathbb{Z}}_\ell$, with $1(\Theta_\emptyset) \neq 0$ for the trivial character $1$;
4. the *norm relation*: let $P \notin Q$ with $Q \cup \{P\}$ auxiliary for $S_0$, let $\psi$ be a character for $Q$, and let $\varphi$ be a Frobenius element at a place $A$ of $\Omega$ above $P$. Then
$$\psi(\Theta_{Q \cup \{P\}}) = \psi(\Theta_Q) \cdot \mathrm{Eul}(\vartheta(\varphi)\psi(\varphi), \mathrm{N}P).$$

Its number is
$$\beta = v(1(\Theta_\emptyset)) + \sum_{e} 2\, v(\vartheta(g_0) - 1),$$
with one term for each entry $e$ of the list.

Lean: `New.Selmer.StickelbergerSystem`, `New.Selmer.StickelbergerSystem.β` (New/Congruences/Selmer/StickelbergerSystems.lean).

In the norm relation, $\psi$ is a character for $Q$, not for $Q \cup \{P\}$, so it is unramified at $P$ and its value at $\varphi$ is defined. Both signs occur in $\mathrm{Eul}$, so arithmetic and geometric Frobenius elements give the same factor. The order of $\psi$ need not be a power of $\ell$.

The sum over the exponents in $\beta$ is the term $\mathrm{len}(\mathrm{exps}) \cdot 2v(\vartheta(g_0) - 1)$. The factor $2$ comes from the two signs. Section 5 shows that on a line of characters each Euler factor has a value of valuation at most $2v(\vartheta(g_0) - 1)$ at a good point.

**Lemma.** Let $Y$ be a Stickelberger system. Then:

1. $\|\vartheta(g)\| = 1$ for every $g$;
2. $\|1(\Theta_Q)\| \leq 1$ for every $Q$;
3. $\beta < \infty$.

Lean: `New.Selmer.StickelbergerSystem.norm_twistChar`, `New.Selmer.StickelbergerSystem.norm_groupRingEval_one_element_le_one`, `New.Selmer.StickelbergerSystem.β_ne_top` (New/Congruences/Selmer/StickelbergerSystems.lean).

*Proof.* (1) $\vartheta$ is continuous on a compact group, so its image is a compact subgroup of $\bar{\mathbb{Q}}_\ell^\times$, which lies in the units. (2) $1(\Theta_Q)$ is the sum of the coefficients of $\Theta_Q$, an element of $\bar{\mathbb{Z}}_\ell$; by the lemma on linear maps it is the image of that sum. (3) $v(x) = \infty$ only for $x = 0$. By the axioms $1(\Theta_\emptyset) \neq 0$ and $\vartheta(g_0) - 1 \neq 0$, so every term of $\beta$ is finite. $\square$

**Lemma (Norm relation for several primes).** Let $Q$ and $I$ be disjoint with $Q \cup I$ auxiliary for $S_0$, let $\psi$ be a character for $Q$, and for $v \in I$ let $\varphi_v$ be a Frobenius element at a place $A_v$ above $v$. Then
$$\psi(\Theta_{Q \cup I}) = \psi(\Theta_Q) \prod_{v \in I} \mathrm{Eul}(\vartheta(\varphi_v)\psi(\varphi_v), \mathrm{N}v).$$

Lean: `New.Selmer.StickelbergerSystem.groupRingEval_element_union` (New/Congruences/Selmer/StickelbergerSystems.lean).

*Proof.* Induction on $I$. For $I = \emptyset$ both sides agree. Let $I = \{v\} \cup I'$ with $v \notin I'$. Then $v \notin Q \cup I'$, and $\{v\} \cup Q \cup I'$ is auxiliary. The character $\psi$ is a character for $Q \cup I'$ by monotonicity. The norm relation adds the factor at $v$, and the induction hypothesis for $I'$, with $Q \cup I'$ auxiliary as a subset, gives the rest. $\square$
