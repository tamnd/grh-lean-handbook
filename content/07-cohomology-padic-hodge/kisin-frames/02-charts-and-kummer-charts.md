## Charts and Kummer charts

Kisin's ring is $\mathfrak{S} = W(k)[[u]]$, embedded in $A_{\inf}$ by $u \mapsto [\pi^\flat]$. The source replaces this fixed embedding by a structure, the chart. A chart records the constants, the variable, the exponent and a unit series. A Kummer chart adds a subgroup of the Galois group that moves the variable by roots of unity. This section gives the definitions, the existence results and two tools: Weierstrass division by $\varphi^n(E)$ and an approximation theorem.

### Charts

Let $q = p^f$. Let $\mathbb{F}_q$ be the field with $q$ elements, and let $\Lambda = W(\mathbb{F}_q)$. The source builds $\mathbb{F}_q$ inside the tilt $\mathcal{O}_C^\flat$ (`New.Lift.flatFq`), and $\Lambda$ as its Witt vectors (`New.Lift.WittFq`). So $\Lambda$ is a subring of $A_{\inf} = W(\mathcal{O}_C^\flat)$ from the start.

**Definition (Chart).** A *chart* consists of an integer $f \geq 1$, a map of $\Lambda$-algebras $\mathrm{ev} : \Lambda[[X]] \to A_{\inf}$, an integer $e$, a unit series $A \in \Lambda[[X]]^\times$ and an element $\varpi^\flat \in \mathcal{O}_C^\flat$, with three conditions. The element $z = \mathrm{ev}(X)$ is the Teichmüller lift $[\varpi^\flat]$. It is not a unit of $A_{\inf}$. The element $\mathrm{ev}(X^e A + p)$ is associated to $\xi$.

Lean: `New.Lift.Chart` (Lib/PadicHodge/Kisin/Charts.lean).

For a chart $c$, the source writes $S$ for the image of $\mathrm{ev}$ (`New.Lift.Chart.S`), $z$ for the image of $X$ and $E = z^e A(z) + p$ (`New.Lift.Chart.E`). The ideal $(p, z)$ of $S$ is `New.Lift.Chart.maxIdeal`. The map $\mathrm{ev}$ is injective, and $S$ is a copy of $\Lambda[[X]]$ inside $A_{\inf}$. The Frobenius of $A_{\inf}$ keeps $S$ (`New.Lift.Chart.φ`). Since $z$ is a Teichmüller lift, $\varphi^n(z) = z^{p^n}$ (`New.Lift.Chart.phi_pow_z`). Write $E_n = \varphi^n(E)$ (`New.Lift.Chart.frobE`). Then $E_n = z^{p^n e}\varphi^n(A)(z) + p$, so $E_n \equiv p \pmod z$.

The exponent $e$ is not zero. If $e = 0$ then $\mathrm{ev}(A + p)$ is a unit, and $\xi$ is a prime. The source records this as an instance `NeZero c.e`.

**Proposition (Existence of charts).** Let $f \geq 1$ and $e \geq 0$. Let $x \in \mathcal{O}_C$, and let $v \in \mathcal{O}_C^\times$ with $x^e v = -p$. Then there is a map of $\Lambda$-algebras $\mathrm{ev} : \Lambda[[X]] \to A_{\inf}$ with $\theta(\mathrm{ev}(X)) = x$, such that every unit series $A$ with $\theta(\mathrm{ev}(A)) = v$ completes $\mathrm{ev}$ to a chart. In this chart, the constants are $W(\mathbb{F}_{p^f})$, the map is $\mathrm{ev}$, the exponent is $e$, the unit series is $A$, and the untilt of $\varpi^\flat$ is $x$.

Lean: `New.Lift.exists_chart_of_pow_mul_eq_neg` (Lib/PadicHodge/Kisin/Charts.lean).

*Proof.* We give the classical argument. Choose $\varpi^\flat \in \mathcal{O}_C^\flat$ with untilt $x$, and let $\mathrm{ev}$ send $X$ to $[\varpi^\flat]$. Then $\theta(\mathrm{ev}(X)) = x$. If $\theta(\mathrm{ev}(A)) = v$, then $\theta(\mathrm{ev}(X^e A + p)) = x^e v + p = 0$. So $\mathrm{ev}(X^e A + p)$ is in the kernel of $\theta$, which is generated by $\xi$. The element $\mathrm{ev}(X^e A + p)$ reduces modulo $p$ to a unit times $(\varpi^\flat)^e$, and $(\varpi^\flat)^e$ has the same valuation as $p^\flat$. By the classical criterion, an element of $\ker\theta$ whose reduction modulo $p$ has the valuation of $p^\flat$ generates $\ker\theta$. So it is associated to $\xi$. $\square$

**Remark (Formalization).** In the Lean statement the map and the unit of the chart are compared with `ev` and `A` by heterogeneous equality `≍`. The reason is that the type $\Lambda[[X]]$ depends on the field `c.f` of the chart, and Lean does not identify `c.f` with `f` by definition.

### Charts of a group

A chart is attached to a subgroup of $G_p = \mathrm{Gal}(\bar{\mathbb{Q}}_p/\mathbb{Q}_p)$ by the elements that fix it. The source says that $g \in G_p$ *fixes the chart*, `c.FixesThetaZ g`, when $g$ fixes $\Lambda$ pointwise and $g \cdot \theta(z) = \theta(z)$.

**Definition (Chart of a homomorphism).** Let $j : \Gamma \to G_p$ be a group homomorphism. The chart $c$ is a *chart of $j$* if every $g \in G_p$ that fixes the chart lies in $j(\Gamma)$.

Lean: `New.Lift.Chart.IsChartOf` (Lib/PadicHodge/Kisin/Charts.lean).

Let $K_c$ be the field generated over $\mathbb{Q}_p$ by $\theta(\Lambda)$ and $\theta(z)$. The condition says that the subgroup of $G_p$ that fixes $K_c$ lies in $j(\Gamma)$. So the field fixed by $j(\Gamma)$ is in $K_c$. The definition gives one inclusion only. The other inclusion is a field of the structure `New.Lift.KummerChart.IsChartFor` below.

### Two tools

The first tool says that $E_n$ cuts out the kernel of $\theta \circ \varphi^{-n}$. Let $\theta_n = \theta \circ \varphi^{-n} : A_{\inf} \to \mathcal{O}_C$ (`New.Lift.thetaPhiInv`).

**Lemma.** For a chart $c$, an integer $n \geq 0$ and $a \in A_{\inf}$, the element $\varphi^n(E)$ divides $a$ in $A_{\inf}$ if and only if $\theta_n(a) = 0$.

Lean: `New.Lift.frobE_dvd_iff` (Lib/PadicHodge/Kisin/Charts.lean).

*Proof.* The map $\varphi^n$ is a ring automorphism of $A_{\inf}$. It sends $E$ to $\varphi^n(E)$. So $\varphi^n(E) \mid a$ if and only if $E \mid \varphi^{-n}(a)$. As $E$ is associated to $\xi$, this holds if and only if $\theta(\varphi^{-n}(a)) = 0$. $\square$

The second tool is Weierstrass division in $S \cong \Lambda[[X]]$. The element $E_n$ is $p$ plus $z^{p^n e}$ times a unit. Modulo $p$ it is a unit times $z^{p^n e}$. So $E_n$ is a distinguished element of degree $p^n e$, up to a unit.

**Proposition (Weierstrass division).** Let $n \geq 0$, and let $N = p^n e$. For every $s \in S$ there is a unique polynomial $r \in \Lambda[X]$ of degree less than $N$ such that $\varphi^n(E)$ divides $s - r(z)$ in $S$.

Lean: `New.Lift.Chart.weierstrass_division` (Lib/PadicHodge/Kisin/Charts.lean).

*Proof.* Transport the problem to $\Lambda[[X]]$ by the inverse of $\mathrm{ev}$. There $\varphi^n(E)$ becomes $G = X^N \varphi^n(A) + p$. The reduction of $G$ modulo $p$ is a unit times $X^N$. Apply the Weierstrass division theorem for power series over the complete discrete valuation ring $\Lambda$, and transport the result back to $S$. $\square$

The third result is an approximation theorem. Its input is Ax's theorem, in the weak form that the source proves: an element of $\mathbb{C}_p$ fixed by a closed subgroup $H$ is in the closure of $\bar{\mathbb{Q}}_p^H$.

**Theorem (Approximation).** Let $c$ be a chart, and let $y \in \mathcal{O}_C^\flat$ be fixed by every $g \in G_p$ that fixes $S$ pointwise. Then for every $N$ and for all large $n$ there is $s \in S$ such that the $N$-th component of $y$ in $\mathcal{O}_C/p$ equals the $n$-th component of the zeroth Witt component of $s$.

Lean: `New.Lift.Chart.eventually_exists_coeff_eq_of_fixed` (Lib/PadicHodge/Kisin/Charts.lean).

The idea is this. Put $t = \theta_N([y])$. Ax's theorem gives an algebraic $x$ congruent to $t$ modulo $p$ with the same invariance. Galois theory puts $x$ in the $\mathbb{Q}_p$-algebra generated by the values $\theta_n(s)$. A retraction of $A_{\inf}$ onto $S$ then removes the denominator.

The retraction comes from a chosen topological basis of $A_{\inf}$ over $S$ for the $(p, z)$-adic topology, `New.Lift.Chart.adicBasis`. One of its coordinate maps, `New.Lift.Chart.coord₀`, is $S$-linear and is the identity on $S$. These retractions return in the frame theorem.

**Remark (Formalization).** The basis `New.Lift.Chart.adicBasis` and its index `New.Lift.Chart.oneIndex` are defined by `choose` from an existence lemma. No statement in this chapter depends on the choice. Each statement that uses `coord₀` uses only that it is $S$-linear and the identity on $S$.

### Kummer charts

Kisin's construction uses the field $K_\infty = K(\pi^{1/p^\infty})$. The group $G_{K_\infty}$ fixes all the roots $\pi^{1/p^n}$. In the source this role goes to the elements that fix the chart. A Kummer chart also asks for elements that move $z$ by a power of $[\varepsilon]$. Let $\Gamma$ be a group with a homomorphism $j : \Gamma \to G_p$ of open image.

**Definition (Kummer chart).** A *Kummer chart* for $\Gamma$ is a chart with a subgroup $\Gamma'$ of $\Gamma$ and five properties.

1. The index of $\Gamma'$ in $\Gamma$ is prime to $p$.
2. Each $g \in \Gamma'$ fixes the chart.
3. The chart is a chart of $j$ restricted to $\Gamma'$.
4. Every $g \in \Gamma$ with $j(g)$ near enough to $1$ is in $\Gamma'$.
5. For every $l \geq 0$ some $g \in \Gamma'$ has $g \cdot z = [\varepsilon]^l z$.

Lean: `New.Lift.KummerChart` (Lib/PadicHodge/Kisin/KummerChartExistence.lean).

Property 5 replaces the classical fact that $G_K$ acts transitively on the $p$-power roots of $\pi$. For each Kummer chart the source defines a number $d$ (`New.Lift.KummerChart.differentBound`). It is $0$ if $e = 1$, and $v_p(e) + 1$ if $e \neq 1$. It bounds the $p$-part of the different, in the file `KummerChartDeRham`. The chart is *unramified* (`New.Lift.KummerChart.IsUnramified`) if $E = z + p$, $e = 1$ and $d = 0$.

**Definition (Kummer chart for the field of $\Gamma$).** A Kummer chart $k$ for $\Gamma$ *fits* $\Gamma$ if four conditions hold.

1. Every element of $j(\Gamma)$ fixes the chart.
2. The product $ef$ is the index $[G_p : j(\Gamma)]$.
3. If $j(\Gamma)$ contains the inertia group, then $k$ is unramified.
4. The image $j(\Gamma)$ contains a Frobenius element for $p^f$: an element that acts on residues as $x \mapsto x^{p^f}$.

Lean: `New.Lift.KummerChart.IsChartFor` (Lib/PadicHodge/Kisin/KummerChartExistence.lean).

**Theorem (Existence of Kummer charts).** Let $\Gamma$ be a group with a homomorphism $j : \Gamma \to G_p$ of open image. Then there is a Kummer chart for $\Gamma$ that fits $\Gamma$.

Lean: `New.Lift.exists_kummerChart` (Lib/PadicHodge/Kisin/KummerChartExistence.lean).

*Proof.* Let $K$ be the field fixed by $j(\Gamma)$. It is a finite extension of $\mathbb{Q}_p$, with ramification index $e$, residue degree $f$ and a uniformizer $\pi$. Then $\pi^e v = -p$ for a unit $v$. If $e = 1$, take $\pi = -p$ and $v = 1$. The existence of charts gives $\mathrm{ev}$ with $\theta(z) = \pi$. Every integer of $K$ is a Teichmüller constant modulo $\pi$. By successive approximation every integer of $K$ is a value of $\theta \circ \mathrm{ev}$. This gives a unit series $A$ with $\theta(\mathrm{ev}(A)) = v$, and $A = 1$ if $v = 1$. So we have a chart. An element of $G_p$ that fixes the chart fixes $\theta(\mathrm{ev}(\Lambda[[X]]))$, which contains the integers of $K$. So it fixes $K$ and lies in $j(\Gamma)$. The inertia group of $K$ acts transitively on the roots of $\pi$. A compactness argument over the compatible systems of roots gives property 5. The subgroup $\Gamma'$ is all of $\Gamma$. $\square$

**Remark (Formalization).** The Kummer chart that `New.Lift.exists_kummerChart` builds has $\Gamma' = \Gamma$. Its proof sets `subgroup := ⊤`. The structure allows a proper subgroup of index prime to $p$, and the conditions on later results refer to $\Gamma'$. The existence theorem does not say that $\Gamma' = \Gamma$. A user of the theorem gets only the properties of `IsChartFor`.

**Example (The unramified chart).** Let $j(\Gamma)$ contain the inertia group of $G_p$. Then the chart is unramified, so $e = 1$ and $E = z + p$. The proof takes $\theta(z) = -p$ in this case. The ring $S$ is $\Lambda[[z]]$ with $z = [\varpi^\flat]$, where $\varpi^\flat = (-p, (-p)^{1/p}, \ldots)$ is a compatible system of $p$-power roots of $-p$. Then $E = z + p$ and $\varphi^n(E) = z^{p^n} + p$. Weierstrass division by $z^{p^n} + p$ gives a remainder of degree less than $p^n$. The different bound is $d = 0$. An element $g$ of inertia with $g(\varpi^\flat) = \varepsilon^l \varpi^\flat$ moves $z$ to $[\varepsilon]^l z$, as in property 5. This is the chart of Kisin's theory for $K = \mathbb{Q}_{p^f}$, with the uniformizer $-p$ in place of $p$.
